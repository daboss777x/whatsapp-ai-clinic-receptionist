# Frequently asked questions

**Do you take insurance?**
Yes. We accept Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Empire BlueCross BlueShield, Oxford and Medicare.
We don't accept Medicaid. Please bring your insurance card. If you're not sure your plan is covered,
call us on (212) 555-0147.

**Can I pay without insurance?**
Yes. See our self-pay prices. We take cash, all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.

**What should I bring to my check-up?**
A photo ID, your insurance card (if you have one), a list of any medicines you take, and any
recent test results.

**Do I need to fast before blood work?**
For a check-up with blood work or a full annual physical, please don't eat for 8 hours before
(water is fine). Book a morning slot if you can.

**How early should I arrive?**
10 minutes early for your first visit, so we can complete your paperwork.

**Do you see children?**
No. Sunrise Clinic sees adults aged 18 and over only.

**Do you offer video (telehealth) visits?**
Not through this WhatsApp line. Please call the clinic to ask.

**Can I get my test results on WhatsApp?**
No. To protect your privacy, results are only given by phone or in person.

**Do you do walk-ins?**
Only for flu shots. For everything else, please book first.

**Can I get a prescription refill?**
Please call the clinic during opening hours. The assistant can't arrange prescriptions.
