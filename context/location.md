# Location and getting here

**Sunrise Clinic**
250 West 57th Street, Suite 410
New York, NY 10107
(Between Broadway and 8th Avenue, 4th floor; take the elevator in the main lobby.)

- **Subway:** 57th St–7th Av (N, Q, R, W) is a 3-minute walk. 59th St–Columbus Circle
  (A, B, C, D, 1) is a 5-minute walk.
- **Bus:** M57 and M31 stop right outside.
- **Parking:** no clinic parking. The nearest garage is on West 56th Street (about $25 for 2 hours).
- **Accessibility:** step-free entrance and elevator; accessible restroom on our floor.
