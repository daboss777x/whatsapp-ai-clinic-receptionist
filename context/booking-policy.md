# Booking policy (for check-ups through WhatsApp)

- The WhatsApp assistant can book **check-ups only**, for **adults (18+)**, for the person messaging.
- To book we need: your full name, the day you'd like to come in, the time, and the reason for the visit.
- One upcoming booking per person. To change it to another day or time, please call (212) 555-0147.
- Bookings can be made up to 60 days ahead, during opening hours only (no Sundays or public holidays).
- Every booking is a **request**. A member of staff calls you to confirm it. During opening hours
  that's usually within the hour. Outside opening hours, it's when the clinic next opens.
- **To change or cancel a booking**, please call (212) 555-0147. This can't be done on WhatsApp.
- Please cancel at least 24 hours ahead to avoid the $25 missed-visit fee.
