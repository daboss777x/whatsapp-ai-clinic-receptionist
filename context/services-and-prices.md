# Services and prices

The WhatsApp assistant can only book **check-ups**. For anything else, please call (212) 555-0147.

| Service | What's included | Price (self-pay) |
|---|---|---|
| General check-up | 30-minute visit with a doctor: blood pressure, heart and lungs, weight/BMI, a chat about your health | $120 |
| Check-up + basic blood work | General check-up plus cholesterol, blood sugar and a complete blood count | $195 |
| Annual physical (full) | 45-minute visit: check-up, full blood panel, urine test, ECG | $275 |
| Pre-employment / sports physical | Check-up and the form your employer or gym needs | $95 |
| Women's health check-up | Check-up plus a Pap smear and breast exam | $210 |
| Flu shot (seasonal) | Walk-in or added to any check-up | $35 |

- Blood test results usually take 2–3 working days. The clinic calls you with them.
- Prices are for patients paying themselves. With insurance, you may only pay your copay.
- A $25 fee applies if you miss a visit without calling at least 24 hours before.
