# About Sunrise Clinic

Sunrise Clinic is a small, friendly primary-care clinic in Midtown Manhattan, New York.
We have looked after adults in the neighborhood since 2012.

- **Who we see:** adults aged 18 and over only. We do not see children or teenagers.
  For under-18s, we recommend a pediatric clinic.
- **Our team:** 3 doctors (Dr. Sarah Mitchell, Dr. James Okafor, Dr. Priya Raman),
  2 nurse practitioners, and a front-desk team.
- **Clinic phone:** (212) 555-0147
- **Email:** hello@sunriseclinic.example
- **Website:** www.sunriseclinic.example
- **Languages spoken by staff:** English and Spanish.

## Emergencies
We are NOT an emergency or urgent-care center. In an emergency (chest pain, trouble breathing,
severe bleeding, signs of a stroke, a serious injury, thoughts of harming yourself),
call **911** or go to the nearest hospital emergency room right away.
The nearest emergency room is Mount Sinai West, 1000 10th Avenue (about 10 minutes away).
